Module skill map

Nine core areas: what each module covers, and the skills it demonstrates for migration, operations, and analytics.

Business Warehouse

BW

SAP BW is the enterprise reporting layer that turns transactional data into management packs: BeX workbooks, EXCO / financial dashboards, procurement drill-downs, HR HIP extracts, credit assessment views, and plant daily cockpits (including production and distribution KPIs).

Skills demonstrated

  • Map masters and postings into reportable dimensions and KPIs
  • Validate cutover with before/after dashboard and month-end report checks
  • Bridge ops, finance, HR, and procurement for leadership packs

Migration / analytics link

Post-load reconciliation: DQ pass rates must still reconcile to BW-style inventory, credit, and procurement views.

Controlling

CO

Controlling owns cost and profit structures: cost elements and centers, standard hierarchies, assessments and activity prices, internal orders and budgets, product / process-order settlement, and CO-PA style margin analysis.

Skills demonstrated

  • Cost-object hierarchies and allocation rules that survive org redesign
  • Link material, process, and PM orders to product-cost settlement
  • Period-lock and month-end control awareness for cutover windows

Migration / analytics link

Cost center, profit center, and activity master alignment keeps S/4 postings and BW cost packs trustworthy.

Financial Accounting

FI

FI covers the books: GL, AP, AR, and Asset Management — chart of accounts, vendor/customer financial views, invoice and payment cycles, AR clearing / bank interfaces, asset masters, acquisitions, transfers, and retirement.

Skills demonstrated

  • AP/AR master quality for Business Partner conversion
  • GL document and month-end closing touchpoints for cutover timing
  • Asset master and depreciation / AUC process awareness

Migration / analytics link

Vendor and customer BP loads must preserve payment terms, tax, and bank data or FI open items and aging break after go-live.

Human Resources

HR

HR spans Organizational Management, Personnel Administration, Personnel Development, training / event management, and time-related processes — org and position masters, personnel actions, learning records, and reporting interfaces.

Skills demonstrated

  • Org and position structures that feed approvals and cost assignment
  • Awareness of HR interfaces into time, training, and dialogue systems
  • Data-stewardship mindset for sensitive master objects

Migration / analytics link

Clean OM/PA masters keep cost centers, plants, and approval chains consistent when logistics and FI masters move.

Materials Mgmt — Inventory

MM-IM

MM-IM is plant stock truth: material master design across raw, finished, and spare-part types; MRP and storage views; goods receipt / issue; returns; stock transfers; physical inventory; IM month-end; and inventory reporting.

Skills demonstrated

  • Material master design (plant, storage, UOM, valuation cues)
  • Inventory process literacy for GR/GI, transfers, and PI counts
  • Month-end stock reconciliation mindset

Migration / analytics link

Material MDM is the heart of this lab: UOM, plant extension, and valuation readiness drive LTMC pass rates.

Materials Mgmt — Purchasing

MM-PO

MM-PO covers the procure-to-pay master and process view: local / foreign / one-off vendors, info records and source lists, freight and claim conditions, PR → RFQ → PO, outline agreements, special procurement, vendor evaluation, and service acceptance.

Skills demonstrated

  • Supplier master stewardship and purchasing-org views
  • Source-of-supply and outline-agreement concepts
  • Vendor evaluation and service PO acceptance awareness

Migration / analytics link

Vendor BP + purchasing info readiness protects open POs and spend analytics after S/4 conversion.

Plant Maintenance

PM

PM manages technical objects and work: functional locations, equipment and BOMs, classes, preventive maintenance plans, work-order create / confirm / complete, and PM reporting for heavy-industry plant reliability.

Skills demonstrated

  • Technical object hierarchy (FL / equipment / BOM)
  • Work-order lifecycle and confirmation discipline
  • Link from maintenance orders into product costing / settlement

Migration / analytics link

Equipment and FL masters must align with plants, cost centers, and materials so PM and CO stay in sync post-cutover.

Production Planning

PP

PP (process industry focus) covers resources, BOM, recipe, and production version; PIRs and MRP; process orders and shop-floor control; period-end / interfaces; and PP reporting — the planning backbone next to IBP / Anaplan-style balancing.

Skills demonstrated

  • BOM / recipe / production-version master data quality
  • PIR and MRP processing awareness for plant planning
  • Period-end and interface checkpoints for manufacturing close

Migration / analytics link

Without clean PP masters, migrated materials cannot plan or cost correctly — blocking ops and BW manufacturing KPIs.

Sales & Distribution

SD

SD is order-to-cash: customer master, sales views and sales BOM, pricing and VAT conditions, contracts, sales orders, delivery, plant weighbridge / output interfaces, billing with credit-debit and rebates, and the sales information system.

Skills demonstrated

  • Customer BP / sold-to / ship-to partner-role thinking
  • Pricing condition and sales-area structure awareness
  • Order → delivery → billing flow literacy (incl. plant weighbridge)

Migration / analytics link

Customer BP mapping and pricing readiness are first-class objects in the MDM lab scorecard below.

Cutover pattern

1
Extract legacy masters grounded in module scope (MM material, SD customer/ship-to, MM-PO vendor, FI financial views).
2
Apply DQ rules: UOM, plant extension, orphan ship-to, tax, bank keys, duplicates, sales-area completeness.
3
Map to S/4 BP / material numbers with partner roles, purchasing org, and plant views aligned to CO/FI.
4
Stage READY / NEEDS_REVIEW loads (LTMC-style), track pass rates, and reconcile with BW-style KPI packs.
