Cutover pattern

1
Extract legacy masters (MM material, SD customer/ship-to, MM-PO vendor, FI financial views).
2
Apply DQ rules: UOM, plant extension, orphan ship-to, tax, bank keys, duplicates, sales-area completeness.
3
Map to S/4 BP and material numbers with partner roles, purchasing org, and plant views aligned to CO and FI.
4
Stage READY and NEEDS_REVIEW loads (LTMC-style), track pass rates, reconcile with BW-style KPI packs.
