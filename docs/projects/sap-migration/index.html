Four-step cutover pattern

The repeatable MDM loop before LTMC-style staging.

1
Extract — Pull legacy Material, customer/vendor BP, and ship-to grounded in MM / SD / FI views.
2
DQ — Score UOM, plant extension, orphan ship-to, tax, bank keys, duplicates, and sales-area completeness.
3
Map — Align source keys to S/4 BP / material numbers with partner roles and plant views.
4
Stage — READY / NEEDS_REVIEW loads, track pass rates, reconcile with BW-style KPI packs.

Module skill map

Eight areas collapsed to one line each. Open only what you need.

BWBusiness Warehouse

Turns transactional masters into management packs — BeX workbooks, EXCO views, and plant daily cockpits used to prove cutover.

FIFinancial Accounting

GL, AP/AR, and Asset Management — BP loads must preserve payment terms, tax, and bank data or aging breaks after go-live.

COControlling

Cost centers, settlements, and CO-PA style margin structures that must stay aligned when materials and orgs move.

MMMaterials Management

Inventory + purchasing backbone — material master design, GR/GI literacy, and vendor/info-record readiness for P2P.

SDSales & Distribution

Order-to-cash masters — customer BP partner roles, pricing conditions, and sales-area completeness on the scorecard.

PPProduction Planning

BOM / recipe / production-version quality plus PIR and MRP awareness so migrated materials can still plan and cost.

PMPlant Maintenance

Functional locations, equipment, and work-order discipline linked to plants, cost centers, and materials.

HRHuman Resources

Org and position masters that feed approvals and cost assignment when logistics and FI masters move.

Plain-English gloss (LTMC, BeX, CO-PA, PIR, FL, AUC)
LTMC
Legacy Transfer Migration Cockpit — S/4 tool/pattern for loading staged master-data templates.
BeX
Business Explorer — classic BW workbook / query UI used for management reporting packs.
CO-PA
Profitability Analysis — margin and contribution views by product, customer, or market.
PIR
Planned Independent Requirement — demand signal that drives MRP for production planning.
FL
Functional Location — hierarchical place in the plant where equipment is maintained (PM).
AUC
Asset Under Construction — capital project asset that settles into a fixed asset at completion (FI-AA).
