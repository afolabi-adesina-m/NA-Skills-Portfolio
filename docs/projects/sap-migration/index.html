How SAP modules fit my work

MDM and cutover quality depend on knowing which objects live where — and how FI/CO, logistics, and plant execution stay consistent after go-live.

Master data backbone

Material, customer/BP, vendor, pricing, BOM/recipe, and technical objects must be clean before LTMC-style loads.

Process literacy

O2C (SD), P2P (MM-PO), inventory (MM-IM), and plant maintenance/production (PM/PP) define what “ready” means.

Finance & control

FI/CO hierarchies and settlements validate that migrated masters still support month-end and product costing.

Analytics & planning

BW reporting and IBP/Anaplan-adjacent views confirm KPIs survive cutover — fill rate, cost/ton, OTIF, spend.

Module skill map

Nine core areas studied end-to-end: scope, masters, and how each connects to migration and analytics.

Business Warehouse

BW

Enterprise reporting layer: BeX workbooks, management / EXCO dashboards, financial packages, procurement drill-downs, HR HIP reports, and plant daily cockpits (including cement manufacturing KPIs such as cost-per-ton and period-end packs).

Skills demonstrated

  • Translate transactional masters into reportable dimensions and KPIs
  • Validate cutover with before/after dashboard and month-end report checks
  • Bridge ops, finance, HR, and procurement views for leadership packs

Migration / analytics link

Post-load reconciliation: DQ pass rates must still reconcile to BW-style inventory, credit, and procurement views.

Controlling

CO

Cost center / profit center accounting, assessments & activity prices, internal orders & budgets, product costing (process / PM order settlement), and CO-PA profit analysis — the control model behind plant cost and margin.

Skills demonstrated

  • Cost object hierarchies and allocation rules that survive org redesign
  • Link material / process / PM orders to product cost settlement
  • Period-lock and month-end control awareness for cutover windows

Migration / analytics link

Cost center, profit center, and activity master alignment is required so S/4 postings and BW cost packs remain trustworthy.

Financial Accounting

FI

GL, AP, AR, and Asset Management: chart-of-accounts and vendor/customer financial views, invoice & payment cycles, AR clearing / bank interface, asset masters and periodic processing — the books side of BP conversion.

Skills demonstrated

  • AP/AR master data quality for Business Partner conversion
  • GL document and month-end closing touchpoints for cutover timing
  • Asset master and depreciation process awareness

Migration / analytics link

Vendor and customer BP loads must preserve payment terms, tax, and bank data or FI open items and aging break after go-live.

Human Resources

HR

Organizational Management, Personnel Administration, Personnel Development, Training & Event Management, and Time Management — org structures, personnel actions, learning records, and time evaluation / interfaces.

Skills demonstrated

  • Org and position master structures that feed approvals and cost assignment
  • Awareness of HR interfaces into time and training systems
  • Data stewardship mindset for sensitive master objects

Migration / analytics link

Clean OM/PA masters keep cost centers, plants, and approval chains consistent when logistics and FI masters move.

Materials Mgmt — Inventory

MM-IM

Material master, MRP views, goods receipt / issue, returns to warehouse, stock transfers, physical inventory, IM month-end, and inventory reporting — core plant logistics stock truth.

Skills demonstrated

  • Material master design (plant, storage, UOM, valuation cues)
  • Inventory process literacy for GR/GI, transfers, and PI counts
  • Month-end stock reconciliation mindset

Migration / analytics link

Material MDM is the heart of this lab: UOM, plant extension, and valuation readiness drive LTMC pass rates.

Materials Mgmt — Purchasing

MM-PO

Supplier master (local / foreign / one-off), info records & source lists, PR → RFQ → PO, outline agreements, special procurement, vendor evaluation, service acceptance, and purchasing reports — full P2P master/process view.

Skills demonstrated

  • Supplier master stewardship and purchasing org views
  • Source-of-supply and outline agreement concepts
  • Vendor evaluation and service PO acceptance awareness

Migration / analytics link

Vendor BP + purchasing info readiness protects open POs and spend analytics after S/4 conversion.

Plant Maintenance

PM

Functional locations, equipment & BOMs, preventive maintenance, work order process / confirmation / completion, and PM reporting — technical object masters that keep heavy-industry plants running.

Skills demonstrated

  • Technical object hierarchy (FL / equipment / BOM)
  • Work order lifecycle and confirmation discipline
  • Link from maintenance orders into product costing / settlement

Migration / analytics link

Equipment and FL masters must align with plants, cost centers, and materials so PM and CO stay in sync post-cutover.

Production Planning

PP

Process-industry masters (resources, BOM, recipe, production version), PIRs & MRP, shop-floor control, interfaces / period-end, and PP reporting — planning backbone adjacent to IBP / Anaplan-style demand-supply balancing.

Skills demonstrated

  • BOM / recipe / production version master data quality
  • PIR and MRP processing awareness for plant planning
  • Period-end and interface checkpoints for manufacturing close

Migration / analytics link

Without clean PP masters, migrated materials cannot plan or cost correctly — blocking both ops and BW manufacturing KPIs.

Sales & Distribution

SD

Customer master, sales views & sales BOM, pricing conditions, contracts, sales orders, delivery, weighbridge interface, billing / credit-debit, rebates, and sales information system — end-to-end O2C for manufacturing & logistics.

Skills demonstrated

  • Customer BP / sold-to / ship-to partner role thinking
  • Pricing condition and sales-area structure awareness
  • Order → delivery → billing flow literacy (incl. plant weighbridge)

Migration / analytics link

Customer BP mapping and pricing readiness are first-class objects in the MDM lab scorecard below.

Cutover pattern

1
Extract legacy masters grounded in module scope (MM material, SD customer/ship-to, MM-PO vendor, FI financial views).
2
Apply DQ rules: UOM, plant extension, orphan ship-to, tax, bank keys, duplicates, sales-area completeness.
3
Map to S/4 BP / material numbers with partner roles, purchasing org, and plant views aligned to CO/FI.
4
Stage READY / NEEDS_REVIEW loads (LTMC-style), track pass rates, and reconcile with BW-style KPI packs.
