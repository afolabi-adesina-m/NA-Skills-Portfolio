How this maps to my real work

At Lafarge Africa I worked in master data from January 2018 to October 2021, including the supplier master file. At IHS Towers I was the SAP Discovery lead for Procurement, between procurement and IT, and much of that work was documentation. This page is a practice run of those readiness checks on synthetic data. The supplier master create and change write-up is on the process documentation page.

Cutover pattern

1
Extract legacy masters (MM material, SD customer/ship-to, MM-PO vendor, FI financial views).
2
Apply DQ rules: UOM, plant extension, orphan ship-to, tax, bank keys, duplicates, sales-area completeness.
3
Map to S/4 BP and material numbers with partner roles, purchasing org, and plant views aligned to CO and FI.
4
Stage READY and NEEDS_REVIEW loads (LTMC-style), track pass rates, reconcile with BW-style KPI packs.
