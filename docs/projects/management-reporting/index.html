Where does the federal budget go unspent?

I built a budget against actual report on the Public Accounts of Canada open data, laid out like a planning grid.

What I built

  • Pulled authorities and spending by vote for 14 fiscal years, 2011-12 to 2024-25.
  • Built a variance grid by department with conditional colour on lapse %.
  • Added a time across the columns trend grid, the top 10 lapses by vote and a computed commentary.

What the numbers show

In 2024-25 departments left C$20.5B of voted authorities unspent, 9.1%. The rate peaked at 24.5% in 2022-23. Part of a lapse can carry forward, so it is not all lost budget.

Source: Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat, GC InfoBase, Public Accounts of Canada, Authorities and Expenditures by Vote, under the Open Government Licence, Canada. Grid layout inspired by planning tools; not affiliated with Anaplan.
