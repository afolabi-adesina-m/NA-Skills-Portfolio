SOP excerpt

One page. Written for this case. It is not a controlled copy of a Medifield or Lafarge procedure.

SOP-SM-01 Supplier master create and change

Status: portfolio excerpt · Synthetic data · Not a controlled document

Purpose

Create or change a supplier master record only after duplicate, bank, and tax checks, and after an approver who did not key the record has signed off.

Scope

New suppliers and changes to name, bank, tax ID, or payment terms. Blocking a vendor for quality or safety is out of scope.

Roles

Requester, procurement, MDM team, finance/AP, approver, IT. See the RACI above. The approver is not the requester and not the person who posts the SAP record.

Procedure

  1. The requester submits the form with the legal name, country, tax ID, and the reason for the create or change.
  2. Procurement confirms there is a buying need.
  3. MDM runs the duplicate check on name and tax ID. A possible match stops the request.
  4. Finance/AP verifies bank details from a bank letter. An account number typed into an email is not enough.
  5. MDM checks that the tax ID is present and fits the supplier country.
  6. The approver accepts or returns the pack. The approver does not post the record.
  7. IT posts the SAP vendor. MDM files the confirmation and tells AP and the requester.

Records

Keep the form, the duplicate result, the bank letter reference, the approval, and the SAP vendor number. Example targets for the path are 5 business days cycle time and a 95% first-time-right rate. Those figures are example targets for this case, not measured history.
