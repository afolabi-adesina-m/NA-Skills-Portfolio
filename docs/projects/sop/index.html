Can a simple forecast and plan stand up to a messy demand history?

I used the Cups4U case from MIT to forecast 12 months of paper cup demand, test the error honestly and compare a chase plan with a level plan.

What I built

  • Measured channel trends, the lost customer, the price rise and promotion lifts from the raw rows.
  • Picked a forecast method on one year and tested it on the next, unseen year.
  • Built chase and level plans with an inventory balance and editable cost rates.
  • Added formula cards with worked examples on every screen.

What the numbers show

The chosen seasonal method scored a test WAPE of 12.5%. The plain same month last year rule scored 12.1%, so a simple baseline was hard to beat in a year with a lost customer and a price rise. The plan costs depend on assumed rates, which the app lets you change.
