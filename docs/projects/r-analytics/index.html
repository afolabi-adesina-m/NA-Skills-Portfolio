Featured Wine price from climate & age Holdout R² ≈ 0.79

Start univariate (AGST), add harvest/winter rain, drop collinear age/population terms, validate on holdout R².

Growing-season temperature and rainfall dominate price; population proxies add little once age is present — prefer a sparse, interpretable model for forecasting vintages.

NBA points from box-score rates RMSE ≪ mean PTS

Cross-tab wins vs playoffs; model PTS on attempts/assists/rebounds; prune insignificant terms; report RMSE and OOS R².

Shot volume and creation stats dominate; defensive rebounds / turnovers often drop out. Always pair R² with RMSE against the scale of mean points.
