About

ECC delivery depth, with S/4 learning in the lab.

I work with SAP master data, ERP systems, BI tools, and applied AI to help teams make sense of their data.

I was master data SPOC for MM, CO, PP and FI on SAP ECC at Lafarge Africa from 2018 to 2021, after three years on Oracle JD Edwards. That work covered data readiness, mapping, cleansing, and cutover readiness with IT on master data. At IHS Towers I used Microsoft Dynamics day to day and represented Procurement as SAP S/4HANA Discovery lead. That S/4HANA work was discovery only. I practice S/4HANA migration patterns in a lab on synthetic data, and I publish BI and applied AI demos you can try. I am now a Software and AI Developer co-op at Sheridan College Centre for Applied AI (CAAI).

Based in Ontario. Open to full-time or contract roles, remote across Canada.
