About

10+ years of ERP and master data work, paired with modern BI and applied AI.

I was master data SPOC for MM, CO, PP and FI on SAP ECC at Lafarge Africa from 2018 to Jul 2021, after three years on Oracle JD Edwards. That work covered data readiness, mapping, cleansing, and cutover readiness with IT on master data. At IHS Towers I managed day to day procurement operations in Microsoft Dynamics and served as Procurement discovery lead for SAP S/4HANA, scoping business requirements and master data readiness. IHS stayed on Dynamics, so the work was discovery only. I practice S/4HANA migration patterns in a lab on synthetic data, and I publish BI and applied AI demos you can try. I am now a Software and AI Developer co-op at Sheridan College Centre for Applied AI (CAAI).

Based in Ontario. Open to full-time or contract roles, remote across Canada.
