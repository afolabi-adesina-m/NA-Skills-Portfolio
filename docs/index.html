Project 02Supplier master data · Public sector

Government Contract Spend Analytics

27.7%

fewer suppliers to report on after vendor name cleanup

Data: public Government of Canada contracts. No client data.
Open demoCase study

i Business challenge

Departments typed vendor names by hand, so the same supplier appears under many names and the spend story is hard to trust.

ii My approach

Cleaned 53,265 raw vendor names into suggested masters with fuzzy matching, measured the duplicates against spend, and designed a six-page report for Power BI and Tableau.

iii Tools used

  • Python
  • JavaScript
  • Power BI
  • Tableau

iv Measurable impact

  • 53,265 raw vendor names matched down to 38,514 suggested masters
  • C$13.9B of spend sits with likely duplicate vendor names
  • 179,829 federal contracts in scope

Approach
Team project that combines the three sources into one score.
